  • 3 (Mind) — Receives the reflection and generates new thoughts

The Mind dictates the experiences within the world, and the world shapes the Mind. This is the perpetual dance between the Electric and Magnetic forces of experience.

All thoughts now first pass through the observer and choice-maker represented by the 9 before they are projected and manifest into physical experience. The Reflection Is the Teacher

The world — the 6 — is not your enemy, nor is it some kind of a prison. It is just the mirror that shows you what you are projecting. It is the teacher that reveals your unconscious patterns.

When you experience something difficult, the reflection is showing you something about your own projection. When you experience something joyful, the reflection is again showing you something about your own projection. The 6 does not judge, but simply reflects. The judgment — the deciding, the choosing, the integration — happens at the 9.

The 9 is asleep, and the character is being lived by the simulation rather than living consciously.

The goal is not to eliminate the 3 or escape the 6. The goal is to activate the 9 — to become the observer of the loop, the decider of what is projected and what is absorbed.
