Name Numerology — Expression, Soul Urge, and Outer Self

By Kytholek  ·  Simulation Source Code

"Numerology for names" is one of the highest-intent searches in this field. Most pages treat the name as a slot machine: add the letters, get a destiny, pick a better spelling.

SSC treats the name as hardware. One certificate. Three frequencies.

  • Expression — every letter. Internal circuit. How you are built to output. Popular name: destiny number.
  • Soul Urge — vowels, including Y. Private hunger. Popular name: heart's desire.
  • Outer Self — consonants. Surface frequency. Popular name: personality number.

Together they are the Trinity of Expression. The date is a different circuit. Purpose, if you want that word, is their meeting — Life Calling — not the name alone.

The Chart SSC Uses

Standard Pythagorean 1–9, with two letters held as masters at the letter level:

  • K = 11
  • V = 22

Y is value 7, and it is a vowel. Full argument: Is Y a vowel in numerology?.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does name numerology work?

Each letter of the birth-certificate name gets a Pythagorean value. SSC splits that name three ways: all letters (Expression), vowels (Soul Urge), consonants (Outer Self).

Which name do I use?

The full legal name on the birth certificate. Nicknames, married names, and chosen names are overlays — not the original encoding.

Why doesn't my name number match other sites?

SSC treats K as 11, V as 22, Y as a vowel, and reduces Expression word-by-word. Generic name calculators often do none of those.

Can changing my name change my numbers?

It adds a layer. It does not rewrite the birth encoding. See the name-change article.
