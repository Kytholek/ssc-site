What Personal Year Is (And Is Not)

Is: a cycle layer — where you are in a 9-year spiral this year, not who you are.

Is not: Life Path. Path is the long curriculum from the full birth date. It does not reset in January.

Is not: Theme. Theme is the atmosphere of the birth year — the era you arrived into. Theme number.

Is not: a personality digit you add to Expression, Soul Urge, or Outer Self.

Is not: a substitute for the chart. A year-cycle of 1 does not make you Life Path 1.

If you already love a personal-year reading from another site, keep the insight if it named a real season. Do not treat it as an SSC frequency. The calculator will not print it. That is not a bug.
