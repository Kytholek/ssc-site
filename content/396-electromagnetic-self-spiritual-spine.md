## The 3 and 6 Alternating Current

Within the excluded set, 3 and 6 have their own relationship. Double 3 and you get 6. Double 6 and you get 12, which reduces to 3. They alternate indefinitely. They never escape to touch 9.

**3 → 6 → 3 → 6 → 3 → …**

In electrical engineering this is what an alternating current looks like: energy moving between two poles, neither one ever winning. Tesla, who gave the modern world alternating current, would have recognized the pattern immediately. The Codex names it directly: 3 and 6 trade places forever — an alternating electromagnetic charge, the two strokes of the vortex alternator.

**3 is Mind.** It is Electric: active, outward, initiating. In the [three natures of number](/blog/electric-magnetic-aether-three-natures-of-number/), the odd frequencies 1, 3, 5, and 7 move into space. Three is expression in its purest directional form. It does not hold or organise. It emits. Thought, language, the first arising of form in consciousness. 3 is the realm where forms first appear.

**6 is the World.** It is Magnetic: receptive, cohesive, sustaining. The even frequencies 2, 4, 6, and 8 draw things together and hold them. Six is integration — care, community, the force that holds a system in responsible relation to itself. 6 is the reflection of thought made visible. The world you walk through is not a separate substance from the mind that perceives it. It is the magnetic counterpart of the electric pulse that thought is.

When 3 emits, 6 organises what was emitted. Just as the movement of electricity generates a magnetic field, the creative act of 3 inherently calls the integrative force of 6 into being. Thought becomes world. World dissolves back into thought. That oscillation is not a personality quirk. It is the heartbeat of the electromagnetic self.

If you only identify with one stroke of the alternator, the current stalls. Living entirely in 3 is a mind that never lands — expression without a world to hold it. Living entirely in 6 is a world that never remembers it was thought — care and form without the originating pulse. The vessel is powered by the *exchange*, not by either pole in isolation.
