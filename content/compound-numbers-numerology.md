Most people are handed a single digit. 8. 4. 11. The interesting information is often sitting one step back: **17/8. 13/4. 29/11.**

That two-digit (or more) total is the **compound**. The reduced result is the **root**. SSC reads both. The calculator prints both.

A compound is not a second personality. It is the **interval** — which two currents had to meet to produce this frequency.

## Root vs Compound

Calculate the frequency correctly. Note the total **before** the last reduction (or the total you stopped at, if it is 11, 22, 33, or 44).

- Root **8** from **17**: 1's will and 7's depth forming practical authority. Star compound. Inner knowing asked to take material form.
- Root **8** from **26**: 2's cooperation and 6's care forming power-in-service. Different 8. Same note, different instrument.
- Root **4** from **13**: the [transformer](/blog/karmic-debt-numbers-13-14-16-19/). Creative force required to prove itself as structure.
- Root **4** from **31**: 3 and 1 meeting a 4 without the 13 lore. Still a 4. Not a 13.

If you only read the root, two people with "the same number" will not recognise each other. They shouldn't. Their compounds differ.

## Compounds People Search (That Are Not the Famous Four)

These are in the SSC compound set — not a new system. Short reads. Your chart may not contain them.

**10/1** — Initiation after a completion. The zero amplifies the 1. A restart that is a reset, not an escape.

**12/3** — Voice in a cooperative frame. 1 pioneers, 2 relates, 3 speaks. Teaching, bridging, the risk of sacrificing the voice to be useful.

**15/6** — Active care. 1's drive and 5's aliveness feeding a 6. New forms of family and holding — without becoming the furniture.

**17/8** — Star. Spiritual depth meeting authority. Do not split the inner life from the outer result.

**18/9** — Power that discovers it was never private. 1 and 8 reducing to contribution. The 9 that still has to handle voltage.

**20/2** — Partnership as the vehicle, not a preference. Zero doubles the 2. Sensitivity at full register.

**13, 14, 16, 19** — the internet's "karmic debt" set. Same rule: compounds. Not past-life invoices. Full page: [karmic debt numbers](/blog/karmic-debt-numbers-13-14-16-19/).

**44** — held as a master, not reduced to 8. [Master number 44](/blog/master-number-44-numerology/). It is a compound *and* a stop.

Masters **11, 22, 33** are compounds you do not collapse. [11, 22, 33](/blog/master-numbers-11-22-33-numerology/).

1. Get the right total. [Life Path](/blog/how-to-calculate-life-path-number/), [Expression](/blog/how-to-calculate-expression-number/) (word-by-word), [Soul Urge](/blog/how-to-calculate-soul-urge-number/) (whole-name vowels).
