The Expression number is the **internal circuit** — how you are wired to process reality and put yourself into the world. It is calculated from the full birth name, not the date of birth. Popular numerology often calls this the destiny number. The math is the same family. The meaning, in SSC, is not fate. It is hardware.

If you only want the result, [run the free calculator](/calculator/). If you want to know why the result is what it is, do it by hand once.

## The SSC Letter Chart

Standard Pythagorean charts run A–Z as 1 through 9, repeating. SSC uses that grid with two exceptions that matter: **K is 11** and **V is 22**. They are not reduced to 2 and 4 at the letter level.

- **1:** A, J, S
- **2:** B, T
- **3:** C, L, U
- **4:** D, M
- **5:** E, N, W
- **6:** F, O, X
- **7:** G, P, Y
- **8:** H, Q, Z
- **9:** I, R
- **11:** K
- **22:** V

Y is a letter like any other in this chart (value 7). It is treated as a **vowel** when you calculate Soul Urge. For Expression you count every letter.

## The Method

1. Write the **full birth-certificate name**, including every given name and surname. Drop hyphens, apostrophes, and numbers — letters only.
2. Convert each letter to its SSC value.
3. **Reduce each word separately** to a single digit, or stop at a master number (11, 22, 33, 44).
4. Add the reduced word totals together.
5. Reduce that sum the same way. That root is the Expression number. The pre-reduction total of the words is the compound.

Word-by-word reduction is not optional in SSC. Adding every letter in the whole name into one giant pile, then reducing once, can produce a different compound — and sometimes a different root.

## Worked Example

Name: **Maria Lopez**

**MARIA:** M4 + A1 + R9 + I9 + A1 = 24 → 2+4 = **6**

**LOPEZ:** L3 + O6 + P7 + E5 + Z8 = 29 → 2+9 = **11** (master — stop)

Word totals: 6 + 11 = **17**. Reduce: 1+7 = **8**.

Expression is **8**. Compound is **17/8** — spiritual depth meeting practical authority, resolving as power. The root tells you the frequency. The compound tells you how it was formed.

## Master Numbers

Do not reduce 11, 22, 33, or 44. A word that totals 29 becomes 11, and you keep 11. A final Expression of 22 is Expression 22, not Expression 4. Both layers are live: the master current and its root.

If your hand math lands on 44, keep it. The SSC calculator does.

## Common Mistakes

**Using the wrong name.** The encoded document is the birth certificate. A married name, a professional name, a nickname you have answered to for twenty years — those are overlays. They matter socially. They are not the original parameters. See [birth name vs known name](/blog/birth-name-vs-known-name-numerology/).

**Using a generic 1–9 chart.** If you treat K as 2 and V as 4, your total will drift from the SSC calculator whenever those letters appear.

**Skipping word reduction.** “Add all letters, reduce once” is a different method. SSC reduces each word first.

**Stopping too early — or too late.** Reduce until you have a single digit unless you hit 11, 22, 33, or 44.
