English teachers say Y is "sometimes" a vowel. A lot of numerology copied the hedge: count Y when it *sounds* like a vowel, skip it when it sounds like a consonant.

SSC does not hedge. **Y is a vowel. Always.**

It is value **7**. It belongs in [Soul Urge](/blog/how-to-calculate-soul-urge-number/) (heart's desire). It does **not** belong in [Outer Self](/blog/how-to-calculate-personality-number/) (personality number). [Expression](/blog/how-to-calculate-expression-number/) counts it either way, because Expression counts every letter.

If you skip Y in Soul Urge, you are reading a different hunger than the calculator.

## Why Not "Sometimes"

Phonetic Y is a pronunciation rule. The name on the certificate is a **letter sequence**. SSC reads letters. It does not listen to how your family said the name in 1994 versus how you say it now.

"Sometimes Y" charts also fail a practical test: two practitioners will disagree on the same name. That is not depth. That is noise.

## Worked Example

Name: **Kyle**

Letters: K=11, Y=7, L=3, E=5

**Soul Urge (vowels Y, E):** 7+5 = 12 → **3**

If you treat Y as a consonant, Soul Urge is E only: **5**. Wrong hunger.

**Outer Self (consonants K, L):** 11+3 = 14 → **5**

If you dump Y into consonants, Outer is 11+7+3 = 21 → **3**. Wrong surface.

**Expression (whole word):** 11+7+3+5 = 26 → **8**

Expression does not care about the vowel fight. Soul Urge and Outer Self do. One letter moved both.

A name with two Y's (Yolanda, Yvette, Maryanne) will drift further. Check the certificate spelling, not the nickname.

## Where It Sits

Vowels: A1, E5, I9, O6, U3, **Y7**

Consonants: everything else, including **K=11** and **V=22**

That split is the [name architecture](/blog/name-numerology/). Mis-filing Y is the most common reason a hand total "doesn't match SSC" after someone already learned the 1–9 grid.
