"Numerology for names" is one of the highest-intent searches in this field. Most pages treat the name as a slot machine: add the letters, get a destiny, pick a better spelling.

SSC treats the name as **hardware**. One certificate. Three frequencies.

- **Expression** — every letter. Internal circuit. How you are built to output. Popular name: destiny number.
- **Soul Urge** — vowels, including Y. Private hunger. Popular name: heart's desire.
- **Outer Self** — consonants. Surface frequency. Popular name: personality number.

Together they are the [Trinity of Expression](/blog/trinity-of-expression-numerology/). The date is a different circuit. Purpose, if you want that word, is their meeting — [Life Calling](/blog/life-calling-number-numerology/) — not the name alone.

## The Chart SSC Uses

Standard Pythagorean 1–9, with two letters held as masters at the letter level:

- **K = 11**
- **V = 22**

Y is value **7**, and it is a **vowel**. Full argument: [Is Y a vowel in numerology?](/blog/is-y-a-vowel-in-numerology/).

## Three Methods, One Name

**Expression** — reduce **each word**, then add the word totals, then reduce. [How to calculate Expression](/blog/how-to-calculate-expression-number/).

**Soul Urge** — vowels only, **whole name**, one sum. [How to calculate Soul Urge](/blog/how-to-calculate-soul-urge-number/).

**Outer Self** — consonants only, **whole name**, one sum. [How to calculate personality / Outer Self](/blog/how-to-calculate-personality-number/).

Worked name: **Maria Lopez**

- Expression: MARIA 24→6, LOPEZ 29→11, 6+11=17→**8**
- Soul Urge vowels A,I,A,O,E = 22 (master — stop)
- Outer consonants M,R,L,P,Z = 31→**4**

Same letters. Three claims. If a "name numerology calculator" gives you one digit and a career, it flattened the architecture.

## Which Name

The encoding is the **birth certificate**. Later names run alongside it. They do not replace it.

[Birth name vs known name](/blog/birth-name-vs-known-name-numerology/). [Name-change](/blog/name-change-numerology-simulation/) if you are trying to rewrite the simulation with paperwork.

Do not use a name-numerology tool to pick a baby name that "fixes" a Life Path. The date is still the curriculum. You would be swapping hardware and leaving the lesson intact.

## Why Other Sites Disagree

They used Chaldean values, reduced K and V, skipped Y, or added every letter in one pile instead of reducing words for Expression.
