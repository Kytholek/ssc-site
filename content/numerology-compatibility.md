Search "numerology compatibility" and you will get a grid. Life Path 2 and 8 are hard. 3 and 5 are fun. 6 wants a home. 1 should not date 1.

That is astrology-cosplay with fewer planets. It treats one frequency as a personality, then matches personalities. SSC does not.

A relationship is **two simulations in one field**. Each person is running seven frequencies. Matching the Life Path digits and calling it fate is how you miss the actual collision.

## What a Matching Table Cannot See

**Life Path** is the external curriculum — what life keeps delivering. Two 8s are not "power couple." They are two people enrolled in power, consequence, and self-governance, sharing a kitchen. That can produce an empire or a war. The number does not decide.

**Expression** is hardware. A 3 Expression next to a 4 Expression is voice next to structure. The 3 will experience the 4 as a lid. The 4 will experience the 3 as a leak. That is not a verdict. It is the instrument mix.

**Soul Urge** is private hunger. Two people can share a Life Path and starve each other because the vowels disagree. A 5 Urge in a 6 Path household will keep packing a bag. Read [heart's desire vs Soul Urge](/blog/hearts-desire-vs-soul-urge-numerology/) before you blame the other person for "not understanding you."

**Life Calling** is the output when Path and Expression actually meet. Two Callings that both demand 1-space will compete for oxygen. Two Callings that both demand 2-space can dissolve into a fog of care. [How Life Calling is calculated](/blog/life-calling-number-numerology/) — concatenated, not added.

Outer Self is the first impression. People pick partners on Outer and then live with Soul Urge. That bait-and-switch is so common it has a name in this system: you dated the consonants.

## How to Read Two Charts

1. Calculate both people correctly. Same method. Birth-certificate names. [Beginners](/blog/numerology-for-beginners/).
2. Put Life Path next to Life Path. Name the *lessons* that will keep arriving in the relationship — not who is "the leader."
3. Put Expression next to Expression. Name the hardware conflict: speed vs build, voice vs silence, power vs care.
4. Put Soul Urge next to Soul Urge. This is where people feel unseen.
5. Look at Life Calling. Is the relationship an environment where both outputs can exist, or does one chart require the other to shrink?

Do not average the numbers. Do not "reduce the couple to a single digit." That is a party trick.

## The Combinations People Ask About

**2 and 8.** Magnetic and electric. The 2 is asked to stay connected without disappearing. The 8 is asked to hold power without turning the 2 into staff. Compatibility is not the question. Integrity is.

**4 and 5.** Foundation and motion. The house wants a schedule. The 5 wants a door. If the 4 uses structure as a cage, the 5 will become the escape artist. If the 5 uses freedom as absence, the 4 will become the warden. Same frequencies, opposite movies.

**1 and 1.** Two initiation curricula. Someone has to go second sometimes. If neither can, you do not have a partnership. You have two launches.

**6 and 9.** Care and completion. Beautiful until the 6 cannot stop holding and the 9 cannot stop leaving. The work is release without abandonment.

None of these are "avoid." They are **specific homework**. [Shadow](/blog/shadow-side-of-numerology-numbers/) of each root is what the relationship will harvest if you refuse the lesson.

## What This Is Not

**Not a permission slip to leave.** "Our numbers don't match" is usually an excuse for a conversation you already did not want to have.

**Not a spell to make someone stay.** You cannot numerology someone into feeding your Soul Urge.

**Not timing advice.** Personal year, personal month, "this is our 5 year" — that is not free DIY on this site. Two charts are enough work without importing a calendar you did not calculate in SSC.

Run both blueprints. Read the friction as architecture. Then decide like an adult.
